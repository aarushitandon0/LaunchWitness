## What shipped

**Part A**
- `app/payclaims.py`, table `PaymentClaim` (one row per order), `Order.status_token`.
- Shop host: `GET /order/<token>`, `POST /order/<token>/paid` (multipart, no JS; 303 back to the status page).
- UTR: exactly 12 ASCII digits, spaces and dashes accepted. Screenshot: `photos.clean_image` (new shared gate extracted from the photo pipeline): 4 MB cap, content sniff JPEG/PNG/WebP, 40 MP cap, EXIF-upright, re-encoded from pixels as one 900 px WebP.
- Rate limits: 6 submits per order, 10 per visitor per hour. A second submit replaces the first (audited as "replaced an earlier claim"); the owner is emailed once.
- Owner: Orders page "Customer says paid · UTR 1234 5678 9012 · View screenshot" (`GET /api/businesses/{id}/orders/{oid}/payment-screenshot`: owner gate, app host only, `private, no-store`, sandboxed CSP). Wording is "says paid" everywhere; only her status button makes it Paid.
- Email through the outbox (`kind=payment_claim`, body emptied once sent, no `order_id` so the order's own email status is untouched).
- Privacy: UTR/screenshot never in audit rows (only "customer says paid[, with a screenshot]"), evidence, report, logs, public pages. Deleted with the order/shop/account (`_purge_extra`) and 30 days after the order is Done/Cancelled (`purge_old`, daily job + once at start-up). Shop privacy page updated.

**Part B**
- `app/sites/motifs.py`: 18 motifs (3 per theme) and 3 arrangements (scatter / corner / band), seeded from the shop name (sha256), drawn as SVG from numbers only. `Profile.motif` / `Profile.art` (keys, validated per theme, "" = chosen by code). `PUT /api/businesses/{id}/signature` (owner, audited `signature_changed`); a theme change drops a foreign motif. Look picker shows live thumbnails.
- `templates/js/fx.js` (one more fixed, hash-allowed script) + `templates/css/fx.css`: add-to-basket fly + bump, quick-view `<dialog>` sheet (stepper drives the real quantity box), scroll-spy sticky category bar, reveal on scroll, motif drift (paused off screen), New-badge shimmer once. Progressive: JS off = the page and form exactly as before; `prefers-reduced-motion` = no movement.

## Decisions (unattended)

1. **Status page** (`/order/<token>`) was needed (none existed). Token: `secrets.token_urlsafe(24)`, separate from the client's form token; test orders have none.
2. **Claim is only ever a claim.** A claim can be sent while the order is New/Confirmed/Paid, not after Done/Cancelled; only when there was something to pay now.
3. **Smaller safe version for "pay on delivery":** S15 says that order asks for nothing now and shows no `upi://` link. Before S20 those customers could still read the UPI ID on the shop page; now they can't, so the shop page says "ask the shop on WhatsApp for its UPI details". No new payment path was invented.
4. **Motif opacity .08** (S20B: plus a clearly visible strip under the hero at .3, where no text sits: `motifs.band_svg`, `.motif-band`) (the CSS value; an earlier draft of this note said .09). The AA test (every theme × accent × background × ink/ink2/muted, worst-case motif pixel) fails at the first guess (.17) and at .12; .08 with shapes at ≤80% passes. Subtle by design.
5. UTR regex uses `[0-9]`, not `\d` (a test found `\d` accepts Arabic-Indic digits).
6. Per-visitor claim limit uses the claim row's `updated_at`, not a new table.
7. The screenshot is stored as one 900 px WebP in the DB (not two widths).
8. **Gifts "build your box" not built** (explicitly optional, "only if time allows").
9. The recording is a filmstrip PNG (`add-to-basket-and-quick-view-filmstrip.png`): this sandbox has no working video encoder.
10. Existing tests that encoded the old behaviour were changed on purpose: UPI absent from the shop page (`test_m2_site`, `test_s8_shop`, `test_s14_themes`, `test_s19d_live_contact`), two fixed scripts instead of one (`test_s10_xss`, `test_s14_themes`), the access matrix skips the 200-read of the screenshot route (it 404s until a claim has one; covered in `test_s20_payments`).
