    Privacy

    How {{ p.name }} uses your details.

    What we collect. When you place an order on this site we collect your name, your phone number, what you ordered, the date you want it and any note you add. If you choose delivery or shipping, we also collect your delivery address and PIN code. We don't use cookies for tracking and we don't run ads. We never ask for Aadhaar, PAN or bank details.

    Why. Only to prepare your order and contact you about it (to confirm timing, delivery and payment).

    Who sees it. Your order, including your delivery address, is sent by email to {{ p.name }} and shown to {{ p.name }} on their orders page. Nobody else can see your address: it is never shown on this website, and it is not in the reports or logs that LaunchWitness keeps about the shop. It is stored securely by the service that runs this website, LaunchWitness, so the order isn't lost. We never sell or share your details for marketing.

    Payments. This site does not take payments. If you pay by UPI, the payment happens in your own UPI app, and the shop's UPI details appear only after you place an order. If you tell the shop you paid, we keep the 12-digit UPI reference number you type and, if you add one, your screenshot (we re-save it as a plain picture, so nothing hidden in the file such as a location travels with it). Only {{ p.name }} can see them, on their orders page. The LaunchWitness team can also open a screenshot when it is helping the shop with a problem, and each time it does, that is logged. They are never shown on this website, and they are not in the reports or logs that LaunchWitness keeps. Telling us you paid does not mark your order paid: the shop checks its own UPI app first.

    Reference photos. If you add a reference photo to a custom order, only {{ p.name }} can see it, on their orders page; it is kept for your order and deleted 30 days after your order is done or cancelled.

    How long. Only as long as needed to handle your order and keep the shop's records. Your UPI reference number and screenshot are deleted 30 days after your order is done or cancelled. If the shop is deleted, its orders, including addresses, references and screenshots, are deleted with it.

    Your choice. To see, correct or delete your details, contact us{% if p.phone %} on {{ p.phone }}{% endif %}{% if whatsapp_url %} or on WhatsApp{% endif %}.
